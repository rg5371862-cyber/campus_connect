Need Clarity?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in Sushant 2026?

All undergraduate, postgraduate students, polytechnic scholars, and affiliated club delegations across the country are eligible.

Is there an entry fee for spectators?

Spectator passes for tech exhibitions and open grounds are free upon digital registration. Select concerts have tiered pass options.

How will hackathon shortlists be declared?

Shortlists are published on Step 4 of the portal and emailed to team leads with room allocation codes.

Can clubs organize cross-campus collabs?

Yes! Use the 'Submit Proposal' form above to detail your inter-institutional partnership and budget requests.
