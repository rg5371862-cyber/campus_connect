Registration & Eligibility

Open Access
Sushant 2026 is open to all undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. students currently enrolled in any recognized university or college, as well as high school innovators (Grades 11–12) for select junior tracks. Simply bring a valid institutional ID card at the campus entry desk.
No account creation or login is required to browse schedules, rulebooks, or submit team entries. General fest entry and flagship hackathons are completely free of charge. Only specialized hardware-kit workshops carry a nominal refundable material deposit, clearly marked on the Event Portal.
Yes! Cross-institutional and interdisciplinary teams are strongly encouraged across all hackathons, robotics challenges, and design sprints. A team of 2 to 4 members can include students from different colleges, branches, or academic years.
02 /

Hackathons & Rules

36-Hour Sprint
Teams submit a 1-page architectural abstract and GitHub/Figma link during Round 1. Our jury of industry engineers and faculty mentors evaluates submissions on novelty, technical feasibility, and problem-statement alignment. Shortlisted teams receive an official confirmation code via email 7 days prior to the onsite finale.
Every hackathon bay is equipped with dedicated 1 Gbps symmetrical Wi-Fi 6E access points, universal power strips at every seat, and 24/7 mentorship desks. For IoT and Robotics tracks, our FabLab provides soldering stations, oscilloscopes, Raspberry Pi 5 kits, and 3D-printing slots on a first-come, first-served basis.

Campus & Accommodation

Outstation participants shortlisted for onsite finals can opt for subsidized on-campus hostel dormitories (separate wings for male and female participants) or partner hotel shuttles near Sector 55. Bedding, 24-hour security, and breakfast are included. Request accommodation at least 5 days before Day 1.

Passes & Entry

QR Check-In
Present your college ID and your Event Portal confirmation code at the Main Gate Registration Pavilion starting 8:00 AM on Day 1. You will receive an RFID-enabled wristband that grants access to all keynote auditoriums, tech expos, and cultural nights.
